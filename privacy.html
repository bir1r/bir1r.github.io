Privacy policy

rclone jellyserver is a personal OAuth client used only by its owner, with rclone, to upload backup archives to the owner's own Google Drive.

  • It accesses only the Google Drive of the account that signs in, which is the owner's.
  • Data read or written stays in that Google Drive and on the owner's own server.
  • No data is collected, stored elsewhere, shared with or sold to anyone.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
